# Maison Vaelore — Brand Book & Marketing Toolkit

Oct 30, 2026 · @Emma

## 1. Brand foundation

Maison Vaelore is a high-jewelry house that makes modern heirlooms: 18k gold, traceable diamonds and rare golden South Sea pearls, made in limited numbers and meant to be passed down. Prices start at $1,800 and the signature pieces sit between $8,000 and $45,000 (all prices in USD; switch currency as needed).

### Brand essence

**"Light, kept."** Every Vaelore piece captures a moment of light — a pearl's glow, a diamond's fire — and holds it for generations.

### Mission

To create jewelry of uncompromising beauty and provenance, made slowly by master artisans, for women who mark their lives with meaning rather than trends.

### Vision

Within ten years, to be the name collectors think of first for golden pearls and modern heirloom gold — the house whose pieces appear in wills, not wishlists.

### Core values

| Value | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| Provenance | Every stone and pearl has a documented origin; each piece ships with a Certificate of Provenance. |
| Restraint | Fewer, better pieces. No discounting, ever. No more than four collections a year. |
| Craft | Each piece is finished by hand, takes 40–120 hours, and is signed with the artisan's mark. |
| Permanence | Lifetime care, free cleaning and re-polishing, and a buy-back promise for signature pieces. |
| Intimacy | Clients are known by name, not by order number. |

### Positioning statement

For discerning women aged 30–60 who see jewelry as legacy, Maison Vaelore is the modern heirloom house that pairs rare golden South Sea pearls with sculptural 18k gold. Unlike heritage giants that sell logos, or fashion brands that sell trends, Vaelore sells pieces with a provenance you can trace and a design you will still love in fifty years.

### Where Vaelore sits in the market

- **Above:** contemporary fine jewelry (Mejuri, Monica Vinader, Missoma) — Vaelore is 5–20x their price and never mass-produced.
- **Alongside:** independent high jewelers (Sophie Bille Brahe, Jessica McCormack, Foundrae) — similar price, sharper material story.
- **Aspiring toward:** the grand houses (Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels, Mikimoto) — same quality bar, younger and more personal.

### Brand pillars

1. **The Golden Pearl** — the house signature; rare, warm-toned, impossible to fake.
2. **Sculptural Gold** — fluid, organic shapes inspired by water, light and shell.
3. **Heirloom Promise** — made to last, documented, and supported for life.
4. **The Private Salon** — luxury delivered personally, by appointment.

## 2. Brand story

### The name

**Vaelore** (pronounced *vay-LOR*) is an invented word, which makes it fully ownable for trademarks and domains. It carries three meanings:

- **Vae** — from *veil*, the soft lustre ("orient") that floats over a fine pearl.
- **Or** — French for gold, the house metal.
- **Lore** — the stories a piece gathers as it passes from one generation to the next.

"Maison" signals a house of craft rather than a label. In everyday use the brand is simply **Vaelore**.

### Founding story (long form — for the website "Our House" page and press kit)

Some treasures are found. Others are waited for.

A golden South Sea pearl takes up to four years to grow, in warm, clean water, inside a single oyster tended by hand. Most never reach the colour of deep gold. Fewer still are round. The ones that do are among the rarest gems on earth — and for a long time they were sold quietly, by weight, to houses that never told their story.

Maison Vaelore was founded to tell it. We began with a simple belief: that the most precious jewelry is not the loudest, but the most deeply made. We work directly with pearl farms in the archipelagos of the Coral Triangle, with recycled 18k gold refiners, and with diamond suppliers who can trace every stone to its mine. Then we hand the materials to master artisans who spend weeks on a single piece.

The result is jewelry that holds light the way memory holds a moment. Pieces that are worn on a wedding day, and again by a granddaughter. Pieces with a name, a number, and a story.

**Vaelore. Light, kept.**

### Short story (for social bios, packaging inserts, retail cards)

> Maison Vaelore creates modern heirlooms from rare golden South Sea pearls, recycled 18k gold and traceable diamonds. Each piece is made by hand, numbered, and made to be passed down.

### Elevator pitch (spoken, 20 seconds)

> "Vaelore is a high-jewelry house built around the golden South Sea pearl — one of the rarest gems in the world. We pair it with sculptural 18k gold and traceable diamonds, make every piece by hand in small numbers, and back it with a lifetime of care. It's jewelry you buy once and pass down."

### Brand archetype

**The Creator × The Lover.** Vaelore is artful and precise (Creator), but its emotional core is intimacy, beauty and devotion (Lover). It is never the Ruler (cold status) or the Jester (playful trends).

## 4. Visual identity

The Vaelore look is quiet, warm and gallery-like: deep ink and pearl-white with a single note of gold, high-contrast serif type, and generous empty space. If a layout feels full, remove something.

### Logo system

- **Wordmark:** VAELORE in widely spaced capitals (letter-spacing +200), set in a high-contrast Didone serif. "MAISON" sits above in small caps at 30% of the wordmark's size.
- **Monogram:** an interlocked *V* and *L* drawn as a single continuous gold line that curves into the outline of a pearl at the base. Used on clasps, boxes, wax seals and as the social avatar.
- **Hallmark seal:** the monogram inside a circle with "MAISON VAELORE · LIGHT, KEPT" around the edge. Used on certificates, ribbon and engraved inside every piece.
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the letter V on all sides. Minimum width: 30 mm print, 120 px digital.
- **Never:** stretch, outline, add shadows or gradients, place on busy photos, or set in anything other than the approved colours.

### Colour palette

| Name | Hex | Role | Share of any layout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abyss Ink | #14181F | Primary dark: backgrounds, text, boxes | 40% |
| Pearl White | #F6F2EA | Primary light: backgrounds, paper | 40% |
| Vaelore Gold | #B8955A | Accent: logo, foil, thin rules, buttons | 10% |
| Nacre Rose | #E8D5C8 | Soft secondary: tissue, ribbons, gradients | 5% |
| Deep Lagoon | #1F3A3D | Rare accent: campaign backgrounds, velvet | 5% |

Gold is always a detail, never a flood. Print gold as hot-foil (warm matte gold) on paper, never as flat yellow ink.

### Typography

| Use | Typeface (premium) | Free alternative | Style |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark and headlines | Canela or Ogg | Bodoni Moda / Playfair Display | Light weight, tight leading, sentence case |
| Sub-heads and labels | Neue Haas Grotesk | Montserrat | Uppercase, letter-spacing +150, small sizes |
| Body copy | Suisse Int'l | Inter | Regular, 1.6 line height |
| Editorial accents | Canela Italic | Cormorant Garamond Italic | Pull quotes, signatures |

### Photography direction

- **Product:** shot on Pearl White or raw stone, single soft light from one side, long gentle shadows. One piece per frame. Macro shots that show the pearl's orient and hand-finished surfaces.
- **Campaign:** natural skin, minimal make-up, bare shoulders and collarbones. Golden hour and underwater light. Settings: still water, limestone cliffs, dark linen, candlelight.
- **Mood words:** luminous, slow, intimate, sculptural, timeless.
- **Avoid:** white seamless e-commerce backgrounds, heavy retouching, busy styling, multiple models laughing, logos on clothing.

### Packaging

| Element | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Outer box | Rigid box wrapped in Abyss Ink soft-touch paper; gold-foil monogram on the lid; magnetic close |
| Inner tray | Hand-sewn Nacre Rose velvet with a shell-shaped cradle for pearls |
| Travel pouch | Deep Lagoon suede with a gold-tipped drawstring |
| Shopping bag | Matte black, cotton rope handles, foil wordmark, ribbon tied by hand |

## 5. Brand voice and messaging

Vaelore speaks like a gracious, deeply knowledgeable host: calm, precise and warm — never loud, never salesy. Short sentences. Concrete details. Silence is allowed.

### Voice principles

| We are | We are not | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assured | Boastful | "Grown for four years. Chosen in a moment." — not "The world's BEST pearls!!" |
| Intimate | Familiar | "Welcome back, Isabelle." — not "Hey babe!" |
| Precise | Technical | "A 13 mm golden pearl with mirror lustre." — not "AAA-grade GSP, 13.0–13.4 mm, Tahiti nucleus." |
| Poetic | Flowery | "Light, kept." — not "A shimmering symphony of radiant dreams." |
| Exclusive | Snobbish | "By appointment." — not "Not for everyone." |

3. **Craft:** Each piece is finished by hand and signed with the artisan's mark.
4. **Permanence:** Lifetime care, free annual cleaning and a buy-back promise on signature pieces.

### Brand vocabulary

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Piece, creation, heirloom | Product, item, SKU |
| Client | Customer, shopper |
| Salon, atelier | Store, shop |
| Acquire, choose, discover | Buy now, grab, shop |

| Lustre, orient, warmth | Shiny, sparkly, bling |

### Writing rules

- No exclamation marks. No emoji in brand channels (a single ✦ is allowed as a divider).
- Never mention a discount, sale or "% off". Offer service, access or a gift instead.
- Prices are shown plainly and confidently, never hidden behind "DM for price" — except one-of-one commissions, which say "Price on request".
- Lead with the material and the moment, not the price.

### Pricing architecture

| Tier | Role | Share of range | Expected share of revenue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entry ($1,800–$5,000) | First purchase, gifting, social discovery | 40% of pieces | 35% |
| Core ($5,000–$20,000) | Repeat clients, milestones, bridal | 45% of pieces | 45% |
| Haute ($20,000+) | Image, press, collectors | 15% of pieces | 20% |

### Pricing rules

- **Target gross margin:** 70–78% on entry and core, 55–65% on haute (higher material cost, higher absolute profit).
- **Never discount.** No sales, no codes, no Black Friday. Instead: complimentary engraving, private previews, a gift with the second purchase.
- **Annual price increase** of 5–8% each January, announced to clients in advance, so earlier purchases feel like smart decisions.
- **Limited editions:** every Lumière and Heirloom piece is numbered; edition sizes of 50–200 per design.
- **Engagement and bespoke:** consultation fee of $500, credited to the final commission.

### Services that sit around the product

- Complimentary engraving and gift wrap
- Lifetime cleaning, re-stringing and re-polishing
- Ring resizing free for the first year
- Buy-back or trade-up credit (70% of purchase price) on Heirloom and Constellation pieces after two years
- Insurance valuation provided with every piece over $5,000

## 7. Launch campaign: "Four Years of Light"

The launch campaign tells the true timeline of a golden pearl — four years from oyster to ear — and ends with the reveal of the Lumière collection. It runs for eight weeks: three weeks of teasing, one reveal week, four weeks of sustaining.

### Big idea

Everyone else in luxury sells speed: new drops, next-day delivery. Vaelore sells the opposite — time. "Four Years of Light" makes patience the luxury.

### Hero film (60 seconds, cut to 30s, 15s and 6s)

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:08 | Underwater, dawn light through still water; a hand lowers a single oyster into a net | "Some things cannot be rushed." |
| 0:08–0:20 | Seasons pass in the water — storms, sun, a diver's silhouette checking the lines | "Year one. Year two. Year three." |
| 0:20–0:32 | The oyster opens; a golden pearl is lifted into light; an artisan's hands examine it under a loupe | "Year four." |
| 0:32–0:48 | Atelier: gold being shaped, polished, the pearl set; a woman at golden hour fastening the Lumière drop | "Grown for four years. Chosen in a moment." |
| 0:48–0:60 | Close-up of her face, light catching the pearl; cut to black, gold monogram | "Maison Vaelore. Light, kept." |

Music: a single cello or solo piano, slow. No dialogue. Sound of water throughout.

### Campaign assets checklist

- [ ] Hero film: 60s, 30s, 15s, 6s, vertical 9:16 and horizontal 16:9
- [ ] 12 campaign stills (4 product macro, 4 on-model, 4 atelier/pearl farm)

### Phases

1. **Whisper (weeks 1–3):** No product shown. Water, light, time. Posts count down "Year one… Year two…". Waitlist opens: "Be the first to see the Lumière collection."
2. **Reveal (week 4):** Hero film launches. Private dinner for 40 guests (press, collectors, 10 hand-picked creators). Website opens to waitlist 24 hours before the public.
3. **Sustain (weeks 5–8):** Artisan stories, client portraits, "how to wear" content, editorial features, first paid media flight.

## 11. PR, influencer and events playbook

In luxury, credibility is earned through editors, stylists and the right wrists — not through volume. Vaelore invests in a few deep relationships rather than many paid posts.

## 13. Client experience and clienteling

In luxury, the second purchase matters more than the first: aim for 40% of year-two revenue from returning clients. Every client gets a named advisor and a relationship that lasts years.

### The client journey

1. **Discover** — sees the campaign, a press feature or a friend's piece.
2. **Explore** — visits the site, saves a piece, joins the waitlist.
3. **Connect** — chats with an advisor, books a salon or video appointment.
4. **Acquire** — buys in salon or online; hand-delivered or insured shipping.
5. **Unbox** — the five-step unboxing, handwritten note, certificate.
6. **Belong** — invited to events, previews and annual cleaning.
7. **Return** — milestones, gifting, trade-up, referrals.

### The private appointment script

1. **Welcome (5 min):** greet by name, offer champagne, tea or water. Ask: "What brings you to Vaelore today — a moment you're celebrating?"
2. **Listen (10 min):** occasion, style, what she already owns and loves, budget only if she offers it.
3. **Story (5 min):** show the pearl tray; explain colour, lustre, the four years.
4. **Try (15–20 min):** 3 pieces maximum at a time, presented on velvet, mirror in natural light.
5. **Guide (5 min):** recommend one piece with a reason tied to her story.
6. **Close gracefully:** "Shall I prepare it for you?" — or offer to hold it 48 hours. Never pressure.
7. **After:** handwritten card within 48 hours, whether she bought or not.

### Client tiers

| Tier | Lifetime spend | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client | Any purchase | Advisor, lifetime care, event invitations |
| Patron | $25,000+ | Early access to new collections, annual gift, priority bespoke slots |
| Collector | $100,000+ | Private previews of Constellation one-of-ones, atelier visit, invitations to the annual Collector's dinner |

Tiers are never advertised publicly; clients are told personally when they reach them.

### Clienteling rules for advisors

- Keep a client profile: sizes, metals, occasions, family names, pieces owned, preferences.
- Contact each active client 6–8 times a year, never with a hard sell: a birthday note, a preview, a care reminder.
- Respond to any message within 2 hours during opening hours.
- Every client complaint is resolved within 48 hours, with a gesture beyond what was asked.

### Service standards

- Insured, signature-required delivery; hand delivery within the city for orders over $10,000.
- 30-day returns on unworn, non-engraved pieces.
- Resizing and repairs collected and returned at no cost to the client.
- Every purchase over $5,000 receives an independent valuation for insurance.
